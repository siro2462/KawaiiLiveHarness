# Historical common-instruction probe: completed result

The shorter historical instruction reduced input length but increased output length. Some supplied evaluation distinctions and timing of wishes were preserved more accurately, while immediate reactions became less prominent and repeated explanations remained. This three-case probe does not establish better overall naturalness. The candidate is not adopted as the default.

M17 better preserved the viewpoint and timing of the supplied reaction, but still broadened a single reported occurrence toward a tendency in some wording. M23 recovered a conditional evaluation that the baseline had reversed and made the past timing of a wish clearer. These are source-relative text judgments by the condition-aware designer AI, not independent human quality labels.
